Privacy Policy

Sharangakutir (Sharanga Kutir, Decor Your Home, Sanatani Mart). Last updated: 16 November 2026.

This policy explains how Sharangakutir handles information when customers message our Facebook Pages (Sharanga kutir, Decor Your Home and Sanatani Mart) on Messenger or Instagram. Our app "SK Omnichannel Inbox" is an internal customer support tool used only by our own staff.

What we collect

  • Messages and attachments that customers send to our Pages, and our replies.
  • Basic public profile information that Meta provides for people who message our Pages: name and profile picture.
  • Order details a customer chooses to share with us (for example delivery address and phone number) so we can deliver their order.
  • Comments left on our Page posts.

How we use it

  • To show our support team who they are talking to (name and profile picture) and to reply to customer messages.
  • To process, deliver and support customer orders.
  • To follow up on open conversations, for example to send a reminder about an order the customer started.

We do not sell customer information, we do not use it for advertising targeting outside Meta's own tools, and we do not share it with third parties except the courier needed to deliver an order.

Where it is stored

Information is stored in our private database (Supabase) and is visible only to signed-in Sharangakutir staff. Access is protected by passwords and per-user accounts.

How long we keep it

We keep conversation and order information for as long as needed to provide support and meet accounting requirements, and no longer than 3 years.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your information at any time by sending "Delete my data" to any of our Pages on Messenger. We will delete your messages, name and profile picture from our system within 30 days and confirm when it is done.

Contact

Sharangakutir, Chattogram, Bangladesh. Message us on the Sharanga kutir Facebook Page.
